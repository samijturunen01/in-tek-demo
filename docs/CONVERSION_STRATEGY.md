# Conversion strategy – in-TEK Oy redesign

## Business model (verified vs. assumed)

- **Verified:** in-TEK repairs car electronics (instrument clusters, ABS and airbag units, electric ignition and steering locks, other control units) and makes, programs and repairs car keys for all makes. Work is done at Kuurnankatu 40, Joensuu. Units can also be mailed in (Matkahuolto/Posti). Customers are private people and businesses. Prices and appointments are handled by phone or email.
- **Assumption:** revenue comes from two flows. (a) **Local key and diagnostics work**: walk-in or by appointment, often urgent. (b) **Nationwide mail-in module repair**: the customer compares providers and sends the unit. Both start with a phone call, an email or a message.

## Primary customer segments

| Segment | Need / trigger | Main objections | Preferred contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Local driver (Joensuu, North Karelia) – keys | Lost all keys, needs a spare, broken shell or remote | "Can they do my make? How fast? Do I have to go to the dealer?" | Phone, visit |
| B. Car owner anywhere in Finland – module fault | Warning light on (ABS/airbag), cluster dead, ignition lock fault; the dealer quotes a new part | "Is repair possible? Is it safe to post? How long am I without the car? What if it fails?" | Email, form, phone |
| C. Workshops and businesses | Need a reliable subcontractor for module repair and key programming | Turnaround, warranty, a simple process | Phone, email |
| D. Other vehicles | Clusters for microcars, boats, heavy vehicles, snowmobiles, motorcycles | "Do they handle my vehicle type?" | Email, phone |

## Main pain points

Warning light that won't go out · car won't start or steering is locked · lost keys · fear of an expensive dealer replacement · uncertainty about posting a fragile unit · not knowing which service applies to the symptom.

## Verified business strengths

Since 2008 · all makes · typical faults in 1–3 working days · airbag unit usually about 2 h, ignition lock within 1 working day, key switch or transmitter even within an hour · unit returned the day after repair · written warranty of 6–24 months, with the repair price refunded (minus a €40 inspection fee) if the unit doesn't get fixed · wide key stock (12+ brand families) · clusters for many vehicle types · private and business customers.

## Conversion goals

- **Primary:** a qualified repair inquiry ("Pyydä hinta-arvio"). The form collects the service, vehicle, symptoms and fault codes, and the delivery method (bring in or post). A phone call counts as the equivalent primary action on mobile.
- **Secondary:** a phone click · an email click · creating a printable service form (huoltolähete) for mail-in · a directions click · viewing the warranty terms · browsing the key work gallery.

## Customer journey

1. **Symptom recognition** – the visitor recognises a warning light in the "Mikä valo palaa?" finder or reads the hero.
2. **Relevance** – the service page answers "Can you fix my unit or make?" (manufacturers, fault examples, vehicle types).
3. **Risk reduction** – turnaround, warranty, the refund rule, and next-day return, placed next to the CTA.
4. **Logistics** – choose "Tuo Joensuuhun" or "Lähetä postitse". The mail-in page explains 4 steps and generates the service form.
5. **Action** – an inquiry form with the service preselected, or a call (sticky bar on mobile).

## Main conversion barriers on the current site → response

| Barrier | Response |
|---|---|

| Placeholder text "Lisää otsikkotekstisi tähän", no footer | Removed; full footer with company details and business ID |
| Contact form asks only name, email, phone, message | Structured inquiry that produces qualified leads (service, vehicle, codes, delivery) |
| Key service split onto another domain | Key service presented on its own page with a gallery; consolidation recommended |

## Conversion hypotheses (to be tested, not promises)

1. A symptom-led entry ("Mikä valo palaa?") helps visitors who don't know the name of the unit find the right service, and should increase service-page visits leading to inquiries.
2. Showing turnaround, warranty and the refund rule next to CTAs should reduce hesitation before mailing a unit.
3. An online service form generator removes the PDF printing friction and should increase mail-in submissions.
4. A sticky call bar on mobile should increase calls from urgent key customers.
5. Authentic key photos by brand should increase trust and key inquiries.
6. Structured inquiry fields should improve lead quality (fewer back-and-forth questions).
