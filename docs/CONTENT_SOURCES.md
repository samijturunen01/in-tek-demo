### Services (verified wording paraphrased in the demo)

- **All makes** ("auton merkistä riippumatta"). Repair and installation of car electronics and car keys. Also diagnostics and accessory installation (S1); fault finding and electrical fault repair (S11).
- **Instrument clusters (S5):** all makes. Brands named: Magneti Marelli, Johnson Controls, Siemens VDO, Jaeger. Also clusters for microcars (mopoautot), boats, heavy vehicles, snowmobiles and motorcycles. Typical faults: rev counter, speedometer, temperature and fuel gauges working intermittently; display pixel faults; immobiliser faults (car stalls or won't start); CAN bus faults. Warranty 6–24 months.
- **ABS units (S2):** units from different manufacturers (e.g. Bosch, ATE). Usually about two working days. Warranty 6–12 months. Faults: no diagnostic connection / fault codes can't be read; wheel speed sensor faults; ABS pump control fault; pressure sensor fault.
- **Airbag units (S3, S10):** typical causes: wiring breaks (under the seat, steering wheel clock spring) and battery/supply voltage faults; internal fault / crash data lock. Example codes: Volvo CFFF, VAG 65535, Mazda B1231. The code can't be cleared with an ordinary diagnostic tester. All manufacturers. Repair usually takes about two hours. Crash data reset is not covered by warranty (S10).
- **Electric ignition locks and steering locks (S6):** part of the immobiliser. Symptoms: cluster doesn't power on, steering wheel locked, key not recognised, car won't start (e.g. Mercedes ESL/EZS, Audi J518). Repaired within one working day. One working key is required. Warranty 12 months.
- **Other electronics (S7):** engine control unit repair/programming, Haldex control unit, Xenon control unit, PSG VP44 control unit, automatic gearbox control unit, Webasto/Eberspächer heaters, climate control panels, other control units.
- **Car keys (S4, S11):** broken shells and remotes repaired; wide stock of key shells; switch or transmitter replacement "jopa tunnin sisällä" (sometimes within an hour). New and spare keys for all makes, also when all keys are lost (S11). One-year warranty on new keys. Programming needs the car on site or the immobiliser engine control unit. Mercedes FBS2–FBS3 keys for cars older than 06/2013. Mechanical, smart and Keyless Go keys. Key stock: Audi, BMW, Fiat, Mazda, Mercedes, Mitsubishi, Lexus/Toyota, Kia/Hyundai, Peugeot/Citroën, Renault, VW/SEAT/Škoda, Volvo.

### Process and terms (S1, S8, S10)

- Mail-in: 1) contact first by email or phone, 2) download the service form, 3) fill it in and put it in the package, 4) send by Matkahuolto or Posti.
- Typical faults are repaired in 1–3 working days. Otherwise turnaround depends on parts availability (S8); the PDF says 1–7 working days for other repairs (S10). The customer is told if there is a delay.
- If a needed part isn't in stock, the company contacts the customer (S1).
- The unit is sent back the day after the repair.
- Warranty 6–24 months depending on the product. Software repairs are not covered. If the unit still doesn't work after the repair, the repair price is refunded minus a €40 inspection fee. Keep the receipt. Liability is limited to the repair price. Removal and installation costs are not covered. Warranty is void if the unit has been opened by others after the repair.
- Units are stored for up to 6 months, then recycled.

## Inconsistencies and open questions (CONFIRM)

1. **Warranty ranges differ:** general 6–24 months; ABS page 6–12 months; ignition locks 12 months; new keys 1 year. The demo shows each service's own figure, plus "6–24 kk" as the general range.
2. **Turnaround:** "1–3 working days" (typical faults) vs. "1–7 working days" for other repairs (PDF, 2019) vs. "depends on parts" (website). The demo uses the website's wording.
3. **Service form PDF is outdated:** it still says "in-TEK Ky" (Oy since 2021) and contains typos. The demo replaces it with a printable form generated in the browser.
4. **Opening hours** aren't published anywhere. The demo doesn't show hours and says visits are arranged by phone.
5. **Prices** aren't published. The demo shows no prices and directs visitors to ask.
6. **Registered postal address** (PRH) is Kämppäkatu 8, 80140 Joensuu. It isn't customer-facing and isn't shown.
7. **"Accessory installation" (lisävarusteiden asennus)** appears only on the old homepage. It's mentioned briefly; scope to be confirmed.
8. **English version:** the sister site has /en/. Whether an English version is needed is to be confirmed.
9. **Facebook:** no company Facebook page was found (the "Facebook" text on in-tek.fi is a theme string).
10. **Contact person / owner name:** not published. Not shown.
11. **Google rating** (S14) is shown as "4,4 / 5 Google-arvioissa (69 arviota, 11/2026)" with a link to Google. It is not in structured data. Update the figure or remove it before production.
12. **ABS faults:** the original lists "Paalupumpun/ABS-pumpun ohjausvika"; "paalupumppu" looks like a typo, so the demo says "ABS-pumpun ohjausvika".
13. **Crash data reset is not under warranty**: this comes only from the 2019 PDF and is shown in the terms. Confirm.
14. **Shipping address for mail-in units** is not published. The demo tells customers to confirm shipping instructions when they make contact, and does not label Kuurnankatu 40 as the shipping address.
15. **"Mistä päin Suomea tahansa" / nationwide mail-in** is an inference from the Matkahuolto/Posti instructions. Confirm.
16. **Domain:** the key service runs at a separate domain (auton-avaimet-joensuu.fi). Recommendation: consolidate under in-tek.fi in production, or keep both and cross-link (CONFIRM).
